Mission profile

The autopilot applies actual thrust acceleration against Newtonian gravity, circularizes the orbit, coasts for two complete revolutions, then performs a controlled powered return.

This is an educational guided vehicle model: fixed reference mass, no staging or propellant depletion, and no detailed aerodynamic heating model.
